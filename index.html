QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Portable toilet rental FAQs.

Can you place a unit in a backyard or on grass?

Usually, yes, when the exact resting spot is flat and stable and the truck has a safe access route. Tell us about slopes, mulch, gates, wires, and clearance before delivery.

How often are long-term units serviced?

Weekly service is a common starting point for construction and multi-week rentals. Frequency can change with crew size, usage, weather, and site conditions.

Do I need a permit?

Requirements depend on location and placement. Public sidewalks, streets, parks, and certain event sites may require approval. Confirm with the relevant property or city authority.

How early should I reserve?

Reserve as early as possible for weddings, holidays, festivals, and large events. We can check short-notice availability by phone.

What happens when my project ends?

Long-term rentals generally require you to contact the office to request an off-hire or final pickup. Pickup is not automatically assumed unless your agreement says otherwise.
