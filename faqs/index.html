Portable toilet rental FAQs.

Practical answers for Yorba Linda homeowners, event hosts, contractors, venues, and project managers.

How much does a portable toilet cost?

Pricing depends on unit type, dates, quantity, route distance, servicing, and access. Request an itemized quote for an accurate total.

Can you deliver same day?

Same-day and next-day service may be available when inventory and route capacity allow. Call (209) 201-0684.

Do you offer ADA units?

Yes. Ask for accessible ground-level units with a wider entry and roomier interior.

Can a unit go on grass, mulch, or a driveway?

Often yes, if the resting location is level and stable and the truck can safely reach the site.

How often are construction units serviced?

Weekly servicing is a common starting point. Usage, crew size, weather, and rental duration can change the schedule.

Are delivery and pickup included?

It depends on the quote and package. Confirm every transport, service, tax, and final pickup line before approving.

Do I need a permit?

Public property, sidewalks, streets, parks, and certain event locations may require permission. Check with the property or city authority.

How early should I reserve?

Reserve early for wedding season, holidays, festivals, and large Orange County events. Short-notice availability can be checked by phone.
